# Multi-core experiments

Not part of the single-core baseline. `threads`: one inference at a time split across N intra-op threads (CPUs 1..N, OMP_PROC_BIND=close). `instances`: N independent single-thread copies, one per core (CPUs 1..N), started together and measured over the same window. DRAM traffic is socket-wide (uncore IMC CAS counts).

Contention check: 36/36 configurations ran with other users below 1 core of CPU (`raw/contention_summary.csv`); unclean ones are marked `clean = 0` and excluded from plots.
